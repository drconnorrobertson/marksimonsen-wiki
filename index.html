Prepare for an advisory conversation

Start with the decision you need to make and its timing. Identify the business or property involved, the owners and entities, the records available, and the responsibilities already assigned to other advisers. Keep sensitive tax and account information in the firm’s designated secure channels.

  • Ask who will lead, review and implement the proposed work.
  • Confirm the deliverables, exclusions, fees and communication arrangements in writing.
  • Separate an initial discussion from a completed analysis or recommendation.
  • Ask how any legal, bookkeeping, payroll or return-preparation work will be coordinated.
